Patios & Walkways

Bluestone, granite, clay brick and premium pavers set on an 8–12″ compacted base with polymeric joints.

Most Norwalk stone patios land between $28–$55 per square foot, depending on the stone, base depth, grading and access. You get one fixed written price after the site visit.

  • Bluestone terraces
  • Paver patios
  • Stone steps & landings
  • Pool surrounds

Retaining & Sitting Walls

Dry-laid fieldstone, mortared veneer and engineered segmental walls with proper drainage behind them.

We also repoint, reset settled stone and rebuild failed walls — and fix the drainage that caused the problem. Where a wall needs a permit, as walls over four feet usually do in Norwalk, we handle the application and engineering.

  • Fieldstone walls
  • Veneer walls
  • Seat walls
  • Wall repair & rebuild

Outdoor Living

Kitchens, fireplaces, fire pits and pergola footings that turn a backyard into a second living room.

Built on the same deep compacted base and real mortar as our walls, so the parts you cook and sit around stay put.

  • Outdoor kitchens
  • Fireplaces & chimneys
  • Fire pits
  • Grill islands

Planting & Garden Design

Native and coastal-tolerant plantings, screening, foundation beds and four-season color, chosen for Connecticut soil and shade and laid out to look established from the first season.

Plantings we install and maintain are covered for one full season.

  • Foundation plantings
  • Privacy screening
  • Perennial gardens
  • Tree & shrub install

Drainage & Grading

French drains, dry wells, catch basins and regrading that pushes water away from your foundation.

Drainage is half of what we do. A soggy yard or water in the basement usually comes down to where the water is being sent, and we get it moving away from the house.

  • French drains
  • Dry wells
  • Regrading
  • Downspout tie-ins

How a project runs

What happens after you call

One foreman walks your property on day one and hands you the final walkthrough.

  1. 01

    Site Walk

    We measure, photograph, check grades and listen to how you actually use the yard.

  2. 02

    Design & Price

    Layout drawings, material samples and one fixed written proposal — usually in 3 days.

  3. 03

    Build

    Dedicated crew, daily cleanup, and a foreman who texts you progress photos.

  4. 04

    Walkthrough

    We review every detail together, hand over care notes and register your warranty.

How long it usually takes

A walkway
2–4 days
A patio
1–2 weeks
A full backyard
3–5 weeks

Your proposal includes a realistic start and finish window. A deposit reserves your spot on the calendar, with progress payments at agreed milestones and the balance due at the final walkthrough.
