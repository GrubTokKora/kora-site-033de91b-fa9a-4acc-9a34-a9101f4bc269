Our story · Norwalk, CT

Built on Hard Work. Driven by Craftsmanship.

Earthworks is a family-owned landscaping and masonry company in Norwalk, run by brothers Nicholas and Phoenix, with its own crew and a fixed written price on every job.

Earthworks Landscaping and Masonry LLC is a family-owned company built on hard work, pride, and a commitment to doing things the right way. What began as a summer job for brothers Nicholas and Phoenix has grown into a trusted, full-service landscaping and masonry company serving Norwalk and communities throughout Fairfield County, Connecticut.

With more than 25 years of combined experience, our team brings skill, attention to detail, and a personal touch to every project. From thoughtful landscape design and professional installation to ongoing property maintenance and custom masonry work, we take the time to understand your vision and bring it to life.

Every property we work on gets the same care and attention we would give our own. That’s why we stand behind our workmanship and focus on outdoor spaces that are beautiful, functional, and built to last.

At Earthworks Landscaping and Masonry LLC, we’re not just improving properties — we’re helping homeowners create outdoor spaces they can enjoy for years to come.

Why Homeowners Keep Calling Back

Coastal Connecticut is hard on outdoor construction. Salt air, clay pockets, ledge two feet down, freeze-thaw cycles every February. We build for that reality instead of hoping around it — which is why our walls from 2008 are still plumb.

What we build →

  • Built Once, Built Right

  • One Crew, Start to Finish

    The same foreman who walks your property on day one hands you the final walkthrough. The same masons, planters and operators show up every day. No subcontractor relay race.
