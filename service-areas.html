We build and look after patios, walls, outdoor kitchens, planting, drainage, lawns, lighting and winter walks for homes in Norwalk and the towns around it. Each town has its own ground and its own rules: salt air along the Sound, ledge and wetlands inland, septic fields on the big wooded lots. Pick your town for what that means for your project.

Towns we work in

  • Norwalk, home base, including Rowayton, East Norwalk and Silvermine
  • Westport, right next door, from the shoreline to the inland hills
  • Wilton, up Route 7, with rocky wooded lots and old stone walls
  • Darien, on the Sound, where coastal exposure shapes material choices
  • New Canaan, with stone walls, mature trees and heavy deer pressure
  • Stamford, from shoreline neighborhoods to North Stamford’s wooded lots
  • Fairfield, from beach neighborhoods to Greenfield Hill
  • Weston, large wooded lots on private wells and septic
  • Greenwich, shoreline neighborhoods and backcountry estates

The same crew in every town

Wherever you are, the job runs the same way. A foreman walks the property with you, measures, photographs and checks grades. You get one fixed written proposal, usually within 3 business days. The same crew builds it from start to finish and cleans up every night. Hardscape and masonry carry a 2-year workmanship warranty, and emergency storm calls are taken anytime.

What areas do you serve around Norwalk, CT?

We’re based in Norwalk and work throughout Fairfield County: Westport, Wilton, Darien, New Canaan, Stamford, Fairfield, Weston and Greenwich. If you’re within about 30 minutes of our yard, we can be on site.

Do you charge for site walks outside Norwalk?

No. The site walk and estimate are free in every town we serve, and the itemized proposal follows within 3 business days.

My town isn’t listed. Can you still help?

Call or text 203-858-1773 with your address. If you’re within about 30 minutes of Norwalk, we can usually take the job.
