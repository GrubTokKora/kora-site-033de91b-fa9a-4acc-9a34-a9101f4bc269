Answers Before You Call

Licensing, cost, timing, permits, warranty and payment, in plain terms. Anything not covered here, ask us on the site walk.

Call or text 203-685-7355

What areas do you serve around Norwalk, CT?

We’re based in Norwalk and work throughout Fairfield County — Westport, Wilton, Darien, New Canaan, Stamford, Fairfield, Weston and Greenwich. If you’re within about 30 minutes of our yard, we can be on site.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Earthworks is fully licensed in Connecticut and carries general liability plus workers’ compensation coverage. We’re happy to send certificates before work begins.

How much does a stone patio cost?

Most Norwalk patios land between $28 and $55 per square foot depending on the stone, base depth, grading and access. We give a fixed written price after a site visit — no hourly surprises.

Do you offer free estimates?

Always. We walk the property with you, take measurements and photos, discuss materials, and follow up with an itemized proposal within 3 business days.

How long does a typical project take?

A walkway is usually 2–4 days, a patio 1–2 weeks, and a full backyard with walls, lighting and plantings 3–5 weeks. Your proposal includes a realistic start and finish window.

What’s the best season for masonry work in Connecticut?

April through November is ideal, but we build well into December using heated enclosures and cold-weather mortar. Winter is the smartest time to design and get on the spring schedule.

Will my retaining wall need a permit?

In most Norwalk neighborhoods, walls over four feet, work in wetlands buffers, or structures near a property line require permits. We handle the applications and engineering when needed.

Do you repair existing patios, walls and steps?

Yes — repointing, resetting settled stone, rebuilding failed walls, and fixing drainage that caused the problem in the first place. Repairs are often a fraction of a full rebuild.

Can you fix a soggy yard or water in the basement?

Drainage is half of what we do. French drains, dry wells, regrading, downspout extensions and permeable paving all get your water moving away from the house.

Do you offer seasonal lawn maintenance contracts?

We do. Weekly mowing, spring and fall cleanups, mulch, pruning, fertilization and irrigation start-up/blow-out can be bundled into one flat monthly rate.

What kind of warranty do you provide?

Two years on workmanship for hardscape and masonry, one full season on plantings we install and maintain, plus the manufacturer’s warranty on pavers and lighting.

How do payments and scheduling work?

A deposit reserves your spot on the calendar, with progress payments at agreed milestones and the balance due at your final walkthrough. We accept check, ACH and all major cards.
